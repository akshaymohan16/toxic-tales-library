## Claim checks and source locations
- Design: Methods 2(a), experimental design and Figure 1 caption: twelve independent guppy mesocosm populations, four per treatment, descendants of wild founders; 24 months of treatment. This is not exposure of the same individual fish for two years.
- Exposure: Methods 2(a): measured concentrations 0, 40 and 366 ng/L (means; uncertainty reported in source). Exact concentrations are omitted from the short Reel rather than conflating targeted with measured levels.
- Subsampling: Methods 2(b-c): 180 fish sampled, 15 per tank; 174 completed all four trials. Distance moved indexed activity; refuge use indexed risk-taking. Treatment replication is at the population/tank level, not the individual fish level. Scoring was blind using video tracking.
- Result: Results 3(a-b) reports lower activity repeatability driven by reduced between-individual activity variance; within-individual activity variance did not differ. No corresponding treatment difference in risk-taking variance.
- Mean versus variance: Results 3(c), first sentence, reports no treatment effect on mean activity. We say no clear difference, not proven equality of means.
- Limits: Discussion explicitly states that permanence/reversibility after withdrawal is unknown. Population extinction/resilience consequences are interpretations, not measured outcomes. The content does not assert them as demonstrated.
