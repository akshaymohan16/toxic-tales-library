## Mandatory acceptance principles

Pilot production checks added during TT-004: a midpoint contact sheet can miss a blurred first frame and a subject leaving the crop. Inspect the first frame, an early hook frame, shot boundaries and the final frame as well as each shot midpoint. Choose footage offsets and portrait framing from actual source frames. Check subtitle width using the production font before rendering; a word-count limit alone does not prevent overflow. Speech verification may normalize numbers such as three/3 and punctuation, but must preserve negation and uncertainty words.

1. Select the ecological question, published evidence and usable visual material together. A good paper may be unsuitable for a Reel.
2. The opening must make sense to a new viewer without a caption, earlier post or unexplained pronoun. Establish subject and situation while withholding the explanation or outcome.
3. Use one central question. Each detail advances it. Establish enough normal context for the surprising finding to mean something.
4. Use concrete spoken language about what organisms eat, detect, avoid, build or depend on. Short sentences and processing pauses matter more than clever prose.
5. Each visual identifies, demonstrates, compares or explains. Beautiful unrelated scenery and moving text do not substitute for evidence or a relationship.
6. Deliver the promised explanation in the Reel. An unresolved scientific question may be the honest answer, but say what was learned and what remains open.
7. Separate observations, proposed mechanisms and broader implications. The key interpretive limit belongs in the film, not only the caption.
8. Every spoken and visual scientific claim requires a verified published source and an exact location in the claim record. Full-text reading is mandatory. No invented data, footage, quotes, feelings or field scenes.
9. Before render, require a substantive cold-viewer, story, visual, payoff, scope and retelling review of the planned film. After encode, require a new independent review of the actual export. Reviews must match source and file fingerprints.
10. Automated checks enforce records, identity and holds. They cannot certify compelling storytelling. After owner approval, use the account's own watch-time/retention data where available, completion, saves, shares and substantive comments to refine the approach without sacrificing accuracy.
