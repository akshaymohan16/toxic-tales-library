# Deterministic motion standard

Version: `toxic-tales-motion-v1`. Authorized 9 November 2026. Applies prospectively to new films in `akshaymohan16/toxic-tales-library` only. This supplements all existing science, story, brand, rights, audio, exact-file release and publication requirements. It creates no posting task and grants no new publication authority.

## Production integration

The existing library workflow installs `requirements-motion.txt` and runs `python scripts/build_motion.py`. The motion requirements include the unchanged historical requirements plus PyAV 16.0.1: newer PyAV 19 removed an argument used by the existing Whisper 1.2.1 decoder. That entry point delegates legacy episodes to the byte-identical `scripts/build.py`. Every new production episode must explicitly declare `production.renderer: nature-motion-10`; the renderer version is `nature-motion-10.0`. Before building, `scripts/preserve_releases.py` verifies every legacy source fingerprint and restores missing public QA/media only from the authorized library origin, checking exact existing release hashes. Missing or mismatched archives stop the build before any legacy rerender or deployment. Held/rejected releases stay held/rejected. Never migrate an already scheduled/published episode merely to apply motion. In particular preserve TT-002 through TT-007, their sources, evidence, releases and provider reservations. The old `nature-branded-9` instruction remains applicable to its existing exports; this supplement supersedes it for every newly produced film. Legacy rendering or a branding-only release is rejected for new identities. The existing producer must develop and test source-specific scenes, or select another verified story, without routine owner selection or approval. Thursday companion carousels keep their designed image format and matching visual identity.

Motion uses the existing pinned Python/Pillow/NumPy, FFmpeg, Kokoro neural voice and Whisper caption/transcript stack. It requires no new package, paid API, credits, trial, subscription or stock service. The hard budget remains EUR 0. Normal public-repository Actions runners remain the existing host; do not select paid/larger runners. Existing producer and publisher tasks alone research, review, reconcile and schedule. Keep three Instagram slots (Tuesday/Saturday Reels and Thursday carousel), one Tuesday YouTube Short, 10:00 Europe/Vienna, and the shared 20-destination monthly cap with Instagram reservations protected.

## Inputs and scientific boundaries

Every new motion plan has `production.motion = {"schema_version": 1, "evidence_sha256": "<exact evidence-file SHA-256>"}`. Pin each licensed asset's `sha256` before source review. A mismatch fails closed. Retain complete source/creator/licence/modification metadata and the archived official logo. Use the existing pinned OFL fonts and archive their licence notices.

Kinetic title lines settle smoothly; speech captions remain on the existing aligned narration clock. Artwork-only transitions leave a single caption and logo layer. Transitions into or out of an evidence count fade the whole artwork through a neutral background in two phases: the old claim fully disappears before the new claim appears. Never wipe across, partially clip or overlap numerical claims. Other artwork uses the feathered wipe. Editorial camera crops of reference photographs do not imply observed animal movement. Conceptual cell, oxygen and other ecological movement represents direction or relationships only, never measured rates, molecular counts or reconstructed experimental footage.

The inherited wood-frog, diving-bell-spider and bumblebee diagram templates are restricted to their respective focal DOIs. Other subjects need an accurate new diagram implementation and source review; do not reuse the frog's temperatures or a bee's experiment for another paper. Generic licensed-image scenes and evidence counts are reusable across papers. This is a source-specific 2D compositor, not a general 3D simulation or generative-video service.

For a verified discrete result use a shot with `kind: evidence_count` and `motion.metric` containing exactly `label`, integer `value`, integer `total`, `unit`, zero-based `claim_index`, `source_quote` and `scope`. Require `0 <= value <= total <= 30`. The verbatim quotation must appear in the SHA-pinned evidence record and explicitly state the numerator/denominator (for example “Five of five”). The named claim must have its original source location. The number and denominator stay fixed on screen; only pictogram emphasis animates. A visible scene-specific explanation distinguishes this reveal from a measured time series. Negative findings, sample size, population and decisive limitations must remain in the narration and scene context. No invented interpolation, expanding population, statistical uncertainty or fabricated temperature/oxygen trace is supported.

The schema verifies identity, bounds and provenance; it cannot establish scientific truth from word matching. An editor must still check the full paper, corrections, exact claim, denominator, comparison, labels and scope. Changes to the motion plan/assets/evidence invalidate the pre-render source review and the final release.

## Exact-export acceptance

All existing checks remain mandatory: substantive six-part pre-render review, speech/caption token equality, neural segment ASR, final encoded ASR and critical terms, 1080×1920 at 24 fps, bounded duration and shot length, loudness/peak gates, real asset receipts, decoded opening/shot/boundary/final frames, cover/carousel review, and exact SHA-256 files. A build or automated score is not release approval. No listening or moving-playback review may be claimed without performing it.

New exports also need `motion_review` in their release record, with `standard: toxic-tales-motion-v1`, `decision: pass`, substantive reviewer/date, independence from the script author, and true `reviewed_encoded_frames`, `reviewed_every_shot_and_transition`, `reviewed_phone_scale`. Bind the exact `fingerprint`, `files`, `evidence_sha256`, `motion_spec_sha256`, and `source_sha256` (the editorial source hash). Record substantive `decision`, `finding`, and `basis` for each of `kinetic_text`, `transitions`, `ecological_motion`, `camera_motion`, `evidence_bound_data`, and `caption_sync`. Existing independent branding and final editorial reviews are additionally required; the motion gate cannot replace them. Missing, stale or failed reviews block release.

## Unpublished preview and verification

Run `python scripts/preview_motion.py --episode quality/motion-preview/tt-007.json --output-dir <private-output-directory>`. The fixture is the approved TT-007 bumblebee scientific story with new motion, fresh source review, no posts and publication disabled. It is outside `episodes/`, never enters the production queue, and has no release. The command refuses site/cache destinations, never writes a release or scheduling record, and renders into the specified private directory. It uses the real licensed assets/fonts and neural narration, not placeholders or synthetic speech. Its manifest explicitly states `publication_enabled: false` and `release_eligible: false`.

Run `python -m unittest discover -s scripts -p 'test_*.py'` and `python -m unittest discover -s automation -p 'test_*.py'`. The regression suite pins all historical source fingerprints and release decisions, and exercises motion/evidence/review failures. Fixed-time pixels are deterministic for identical inputs, pinned assets/fonts and software; encoder/model/runtime changes across operating systems may change MP4 bytes, so every new exact output still needs its own release.
