## Scientific checks before rendering

Read the full peer-reviewed original paper. Record title, authors, DOI, journal, year, full-text URL, date checked, exact source locations for each spoken or visual claim, independent replication, controls, species, exposure and measured versus nominal doses, results, uncertainty and causal limits. Check corrections/retractions and relevant subsequent contrary findings. Abstracts, news stories and generated summaries are discovery aids, never sufficient proof. An unavailable or ambiguous paper is replaced with another fully checked candidate. Never invent numbers, data points, research footage or mechanistic certainty.

Report the context as carefully as the focal result. Support every contextual claim from verified published evidence; source count follows the claims rather than an arbitrary minimum. Do not pad the story to cite extra papers. Create the full `editorial_story` brief and per-segment visual plan required by EDITORIAL_REVIEW_SCHEMA.md. Distinguish established context, study findings and the editor's interpretation. Do not invent interviews, quotations, researcher reactions or field scenes.

Maintain `evidence/<id>.md`, the episode JSON, screening notes and a dated batch record. Each entry publishes a searchable paper record and a source/credit page. Link the DOI and archive in each caption. Store legal media credits, licence URLs, source URLs, modifications and download hashes. Do not redistribute publisher PDFs or assume that an image on a journal page is reusable.

## Editorial acceptance

The visual reference is the owner's original account, including the 31 May 2025 desert-locust Reel (`DKUQ1HUMjEB`), viewed in the 7 November integration session: organism-first opening, nature imagery, short shots, prominent readable captions. The rejected eSpeak TT-002 post, v2 slideshow and TT-003 v4 methods/results film are never quality benchmarks. Original audio parity was not established and must not be claimed.

Select a complete ecological story and usable visuals together: a concrete subject/situation, a surprising relationship or puzzle, sufficient context, evidence that answers the question, its decisive limitation and a bounded payoff. A sound paper can be unsuitable for a Reel. The study belongs inside that story. Generic methods/results summaries, including longer versions padded with background, fail editorial review. Avoid forced disaster, medical relevance or intentional-animal language; survival, dispersal, cooperation, deception and dependency are legitimate interests. Methods-focused companion carousels remain appropriate.

Before rendering, `validate_pre_render` must verify a current source hash and substantive observations for all six editorial tests: cold viewer, connected story, visual explanation, payoff, scientific scope and retelling. The source hash includes the script, story, asset references, caption and evidence record. A changed source requires a new review. A filled field or declared pass is not proof of quality; the editor must perform the work and identify actual evidence for the judgement.

The opening should show an organism/process with an intelligible, supported hook and no introductory logo card. Name or visibly identify the subject and establish the situation; curiosity should concern the explanation, not which animals or event the narrator means. The 60-85-second planning range sits within a 45-95-second technical envelope; neither is a guaranteed engagement rule. Use a paired narration/visual storyboard and enough time to understand each turn. Every image must identify, demonstrate, compare or explain. Use relevant organism footage or meaningful motion and focused diagrams for relationships. Avoid long repeated stock sequences and successive slide-like cards. More than a token camera zoom is needed to call a scene animated. Main captions must fit within the phone safe region at a readable size. Keep count labels, methods and citations secondary; avoid redundant headline, paragraph and subtitles. Keep a scientific negation with the claim it qualifies. Explicitly label conceptual drawings; avoid invented data-like graphs or population counts. Integrate the material caveat where it changes interpretation. Fulfil the opening promise within the Reel, with a compact source credit. A trailer for another video is not a complete post.

Every completed export requires actual encoded-frame inspection at each shot, all carousel pages and the cover. Inspect a moving playback when available; compare comprehension, pacing, topic-specific visuals and readability, not just technical success. Read the final ASR transcript and listen when listening capability exists. Never claim human listening from a volume or transcript check. The technical audio gate uses segment and final-export transcription, critical terms, speech/caption matching, approximately -16 LUFS and safe true peak. Pin the accepted voice and retain the transcript/loudness evidence. A material pronunciation or visual problem requires correction and a new review.
