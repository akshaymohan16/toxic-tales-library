- Open with a specific organism/finding/question, not an abstract slogan. Lead with the interesting result without overclaiming it.
- Original strong cover and complete graphical abstract; no repetitive generic slideshow. Use genuine footage only where relevant, licensed, and clearly identified as not study footage.
