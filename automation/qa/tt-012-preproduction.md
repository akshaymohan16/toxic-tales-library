## Claim audit
- A moving current can carry dissolved food cues downstream. Lab flume 185 x 116 cm, 10–12 cm water depth, approx 2 cm/s current; near-infrared recording under blackout tent. Methods, Fig 2, cached lines 42–54. Do not imply absolute absence of all radiation or measured concentration at each arm. Schematic plume extends downstream away from upstream food.
- Three-station discrimination: five O. rubescens (3 male, 2 female), repeated nightly for one week, one baited station and two unbaited, variable position, shrimp/crab. In successful feeding sessions, upstream approaches favored baited over unbaited station beyond geometry-adjusted null. Methods/Results lines 63–66, 89–91 (Fig 4). Do not imply all attempts succeeded, each trial independent, or location memory experimentally eliminated.
- Single-station study: five analyzed males, nine tested, four excluded for presumed low appetite related to senescence. Of 41 successful feeding approaches, 31 arrived from inside approximate proxy plume and 10 outside. Results and Fig 5; repeated events, not 41 animals; plume boundary derived from simulated bead scattering, not direct simultaneous measurement of dissolved cue. No claim that every octopus used same strategy or that 31/41 is individual-level preference. Do not animate invented paths as data. Use a static labelled 31/10 event count revealed as whole art only.
- Pauses, zigzags and upstream surges observed in approach tracks; interpreted as consistent with plume-guided navigation. Fig 6, Results. Conceptual illustrative path, not measured tracks or a universal algorithm.
- Fast arm-aligned movements occurred more during tracking than controls (3.1% of time vs 0.24% no-food and 0.148% no-eating controls), but arm tips could not be individually reliably tracked. No consistent bilateral head reorientation. Hypothesis that suckers guide movement, not experimental isolation of sensory organs or proof that only arms are used. Avoid numerical FAAM in film unless separate event-vs-time distinction can be made clearly; emphasize hypothesis and no receptor manipulation.
- Lab conditions, small repeated samples, successful-feeding selection, exclusions, proxy plume and fixed single station prevent generalizing to natural foraging distances, neural pathways, memory or necessity of any one organ. No anthropomorphic claim about an arm thinking or smelling independently.
