# Toxic Tales visual pilot standard

Status: awaiting owner approval, 7 November 2026. This is a candidate visual direction, not an owner-approved benchmark. Do not claim that it matches or exceeds the owner's earlier VEED.io work until the owner judges the finished pilot.

## Observed reference and changes
The earlier Desert Locust Upsurge Reel at https://www.instagram.com/toxic_tales_eco/reel/DKUQ1HUMjEB/ was visually inspected. It uses full-frame organism/environment footage and short, bold white italic captions with strong outlines and occasional colour emphasis. The pilot applies those useful treatments with the account's near-black and electric-lime identity. Its claims remain limited by the current paper records, regardless of the old Reel's claims.

The previous microscopy pipeline decoded landscape footage to 960 x 540, then vertically cropped and enlarged it. The new pipeline decodes at 24 fps and 1280 pixels high for the 720 x 1280 composition; portrait source footage starts at 2160 x 3840. Source-specific cache names prevent old frames from replacing new footage. Final delivery is H.264 1080 x 1920 with AAC audio. Output dimensions alone do not establish true image detail.

## Review the actual export
- Open with a clear organism/process and a comprehensible story hook in the first three seconds; avoid a branding lead-in.
- Use legible captions with a strong outline, restrained lime emphasis and brief entrance motion. Preserve complete qualifications and negations; do not make the viewer chase animated words.
- Use full-screen, correctly identified reference imagery. Preserve the subject when cropping, and avoid over-enlargement, repeated blurry shots or a photo floating inside a large empty frame.
- Use diagrams for relationships that footage cannot explain: who eats whom, the route of exposure, which pressure ends and which continues. Mark conceptual illustrations; never fabricate numerical evidence.
- Vary shots for meaning, not simply to satisfy a scene counter. The visual sequence should remain understandable without sound and narration should remain coherent without reading captions.
- Check all encoded scenes and cover at phone viewing size, including the start and end of animated transitions. Record sharpness, caption collisions, safe margins, pacing and any remaining limitations honestly.
- Compare the next candidate with the exact owner-approved pilot once approval is recorded. A written review field or successful build cannot substitute for visual judgement.

The complete unpublished review package is generated at pilot.html. Its film, caption, cover, source record and exact file hashes must remain linked. Any later visual or audio change requires a fresh export and review. Owner approval and the current hold are governed by automation/config.json and OPERATIONS.md.
